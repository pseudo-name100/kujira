I · The song

A call that didn't fit.

In 1989 a U.S. Navy listening station in the Pacific recorded a whale call nobody could place. Blue whales sing between about 10 and 40 hertz; fin whales near 20. This one sang at 52. Researchers have picked it up season after season since, crossing thousands of miles of ocean, always at its own pitch.

It has never been seen. As far as anyone knows, nothing has ever answered it. The newspapers called it the loneliest whale in the world.

We liked a different ending. Kujira, 鯨, is that song sung on Ink, and every holder is an answer.

II · What Kujira is

A memecoin that pays the ones who stay.

  • One billion KUJI. Minted once, at the start. Nobody can make more.
  • The Pod. Stake KUJI and share a fixed pool of rewards. Bigger stakes and longer stays earn more, up to 2.5 times.
  • The Drop. A quarter of all KUJI, given away in rounds: Ink's traders first, then the pod's whales.
  • The ranks. Krill, Fish, Dolphin, Orca, Kujira. Every creature has its place.

III · What Kujira is not

Not a promise.

KUJI is a meme. It has no intrinsic value, no roadmap of riches, no team allocation, no presale, no investors and no tax. Nobody is promising you a return, and you shouldn't buy anything you can't afford to lose. If it grows, it grows because people chose to gather.

IV · How it launched

Nothing held back, and one fee said out loud.

  • No team tokens. Not one KUJI was kept by the people building it.
  • Liquidity locked forever. The KUJI/ETH liquidity sits in a 1% Uniswap pool, owned by a contract that has no way to remove it.
  • The 1% pool fee. Every trade pays Uniswap's 1% pool fee, and that fee goes to the builders' wallet. It's how the site, the art and the Drops get paid for. It's the only thing they take, and the contract that collects it is public.
  • Code you can read. Every contract is verified on the Ink explorer, and the code is open.

No team allocation and liquidity out of reach: the way Ryoshi launched Shiba Inu. Kujira isn't connected to Ryoshi or Shiba Inu.

VI · The pump

The sea is richer wherever whales stay.

Whales feed in the deep and rise to breathe, bringing nutrients up with them. Biologists call it the whale pump: places where whales stay hold more life. The Pod rewards the same thing.

  • Size100K+ +0.10×1M+ +0.25×10M+ +0.50×
  • TimeEvery 30 days +0.25×Up to 120 days +1.00×

Rewards are shared by boosted stake, so a 10M stake held for four months earns 2.5 times what the same KUJI earns on day one. You can always leave, but withdrawing restarts the clock. Rewards come only from the pool put in at the start. Nothing is printed.

VII · The fall

One whale can feed the deep for fifty years.

When a whale dies it sinks to the seafloor, and its body feeds whole communities of deep-sea life for decades. Biologists call it a whale fall. The Drop is Kujira's.

Round 1 goes to the people already trading on Ink: 14,981 wallets that swapped on Ink's DEXes on at least two days this year, each listed for 81,766 KUJI. Claims are first come, first served until the round's 80M KUJI is gone. Claim straight into the Pod for 25% more.

Later rounds go to the pod itself. Whale rounds take a snapshot of every holder, wallet and Pod together, and reward those of Orca size and up. Snapshots are announced before they happen, and every list is published so anyone can check it at kujira.ink.

VIII · What the builders can and can't do

A short list, on purpose.

  • Can: add rewards to the Pod, start Drop rounds from the published lists, burn unused Drop tokens, and collect the 1% pool fee.
  • Cannot: mint KUJI, tax it, freeze a wallet, touch anyone's stake, take the Drop or Pod pools, or remove the locked liquidity.
  • Will: hand the remaining controls to a community multisig, or give them up, once the rounds are done.
