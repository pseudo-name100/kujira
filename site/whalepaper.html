I. The fisherman's note

漁師の手記

I am a fisherman. For a long time I worked the surface of Ink and watched the same nets come up.

Tokens with hidden taxes. Owners who could print more whenever they liked. Teams who sold the moment anyone arrived. The water looked busy, but nothing lived in it for long.

One morning something else was in the net. It was heavy, and it was alive. A whale.

I could have sold it. Instead I cut the net. I kept none of it. Whatever it becomes now belongs to those who swim with it. I ask one small toll on the water, and I'll tell you exactly what it is.

— 漁師, the fisherman

II. What Kujira is

A memecoin on Ink, and an experiment in rewarding the ones who stay.

  • One billion KUJI. Minted once, at the start. Nobody can make more.
  • The Pod. Stake KUJI and share in a fixed pool of rewards. Bigger stakes and longer stays earn more, up to 2.5 times.
  • The Drop. A quarter of all KUJI, released in rounds to people already on the water: Ink traders first, then the pod's whales.
  • The ranks. Krill, Fish, Dolphin, Orca, Kujira. Every creature has its place.

III. What Kujira is not

Not a promise.

KUJI is a meme. It has no intrinsic value, no roadmap of riches, no team, no presale, no investors, and no tax after the first 30 minutes. Nobody is promising you a return, and you should not buy anything you can't afford to lose. If it grows, it grows because people chose to gather.

IV. How the net was cut

Give it away. Keep nothing. Let the community build.

  • No team tokens. Not one KUJI held back.
  • Liquidity locked forever. The KUJI/ETH liquidity sits in a 1% Uniswap pool, owned by a contract with no way to remove it. Anyone can read it.
  • No insider ETH. The pool opened at a $10k market cap with KUJI only. Every ETH in it came from a buyer.
  • The tolls, out in the open. Every trade pays Uniswap's 1% pool fee, and that fee goes to the fisherman's wallet; the contract that collects it is public. For the first 30 minutes there is also a launch tax on buys, starting at 25% and falling every second to 0, so sniper bots pay for rushing in. It goes to the same wallet, it's written into the token, and it can never be switched back on. These pay for the site, the art and the Drops, and they're everything the fisherman takes.
  • Code you can read. Every contract is verified on the Ink explorer, and the code is open.
  • No hidden switches. No tax after the launch window, no blacklist, no pause, no upgrades.

The spirit is Ryoshi's, who launched Shiba Inu with no team allocation and the liquidity out of reach. Kujira isn't connected to Ryoshi or Shiba Inu.

VI. The Pod pays whales to stay

Most memecoins punish patience. The Pod pays for it.

  • SizeDolphin 100K+ +0.10×Orca 1M+ +0.25×Kujira 10M+ +0.50×
  • TimeEvery 30 days staked +0.25×Up to 120 days +1.00×

Rewards are shared by boosted stake, so a Kujira-size stake held for four months earns 2.5 times what the same KUJI would earn on day one. Leaving is always allowed, but withdrawing anything restarts the clock. Rewards come only from the pool that was put in at the start. Nothing is printed.

VII. The Drop falls on those already on the water

When the whale breaches, the spray falls on all who stand near.

The first round goes to the people who already trade on Ink: 14,981 wallets that swapped on Ink's DEXes on at least two days this year, each listed for 81,766 KUJI. Claims are first come, first served until the round's 80M KUJI is gone. Claim straight into the Pod for 25% more.

The Drop never outgrows the market. A round opens only once buyers have taken at least twice its size out of the pool, so the spray never lands harder than the water can hold. Round 1 opens at about a $25k market cap, and the site shows how close it is.

Later rounds go to the pod itself. Whale rounds snapshot every holder, wallet and Pod together, and reward those of Orca size and up. Snapshots are announced before they happen.

Check any wallet at kujira.ink. Every list is published so anyone can verify it.

VIII. What the fisherman can and cannot do

The fisherman holds a rope, not a net.

  • Can: add rewards to the Pod (never speed up or slow down what's already promised), start Drop rounds, burn unused Drop tokens, and collect the 1% pool fee.
  • Cannot: mint KUJI, add or extend a tax, freeze a wallet, touch anyone's stake, take the Pod's rewards, reuse a Drop list, or remove the locked liquidity.
  • Trust, said plainly: a Drop round pays whoever is on its list, and the fisherman writes the lists. That's why every list is published before its round starts, with its fingerprint (the Merkle root) matching the one on-chain, so anyone can check it.
  • Will: hand the remaining controls to a community multisig, or give them up entirely, once the rounds are done.

IX. The pod decides
